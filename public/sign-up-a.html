Questions, answered

Will the posts sound like me?
Yes. ScoutHook interviews you and writes from your own words and ideas, not a template. It sounds like you because the thinking is yours. You can edit any line before you post.
Do you post to LinkedIn for me?
You are always in control. You approve every post before it goes live. If you schedule one, ScoutHook only publishes what you already signed off on.
How much time does it take?
About an hour a week. Most of that is talking through your ideas. ScoutHook handles the writing, the structure, and the formatting.
What if it's not for me?
Your first 7 days are free and we never ask for a card to start — so if ScoutHook isn't for you, there's nothing to cancel and nothing charged. You only decide about paying once you've actually used it.
