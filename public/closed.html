ScoutHook is closed

ScoutHook closed on Wednesday 16 September. It is no longer open to new accounts, and existing accounts have been closed.

ScoutHook helped founders and consultants turn their ideas into LinkedIn posts that sounded like them rather than like AI.

Building it taught us something we didn’t expect. We assumed the hard part of posting on LinkedIn was the writing, so that’s what we built for. As things turned out, it wasn’t the problem.

Thank you to everyone who gave it a try.

Hrishikesh and Chirag Jobanputra Co-founders, ScoutHook
